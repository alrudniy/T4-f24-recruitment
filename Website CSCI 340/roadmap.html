Family Promise Landlord Verification Roadmap

  1. Step 1: Initial Inquiry

    Potential landlords express interest and inquire about the program, perhaps through an online form or phone call.

  2. Step 2: Information Session

    Family Promise provides detailed information about the program, including expectations, responsibilities, and benefits of participation.

  3. Step 3: Property Assessment

    If the landlord proceeds, Family Promise assesses the property to ensure it meets safety and habitability standards. This may involve an inspection.

  4. Step 4: Background Check

    Family Promise conducts background checks on potential landlords to ensure the safety and security of program participants.

  5. Step 5: Agreement Signing

    If approved, the landlord signs an agreement with Family Promise outlining the terms and conditions of participation.

  6. Step 6: Landlord Onboarding

    Family Promise provides onboarding and training to prepare landlords for hosting families, covering topics like communication, support, and emergency procedures.
