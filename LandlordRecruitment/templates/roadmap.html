Steps to Get Approved

  1. Step 1: Application Submission

    Homeowners must fill out an application form providing personal details, financial information, and reasons for seeking assistance.

  2. Step 2: Initial Review

    The Family Promise team reviews the application to ensure all necessary information is provided and checks for initial eligibility criteria.

  3. Step 3: Document Verification

    Applicants are required to submit supporting documents such as proof of income, identification, and any other relevant paperwork.

  4. Step 4: Interview

    A face-to-face or virtual interview is conducted to understand the homeowner's situation better and assess their needs.

  5. Step 5: Final Assessment

    The application and interview details are assessed by a panel to make a final decision on approval.

  6. Step 6: Notification

    Homeowners are notified of the decision. If approved, they are provided with next steps and resources to get started with Family Promise.
